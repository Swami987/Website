01.

Even filtrations and stacks

Master's thesis
My master's thesis was advised by Thomas Nikolaus

This thesis is a study of the Even Filtration, due to Hahn-Raksit-Wilson and the stacky analogue due to Rok Gregoric. Additionally, we use the methods of the former to define even filtrations for fixed points of actions of finite subgroups of the circle, and apply this to define an even filtration on TR (topological restriction homology). We identify this with the filtration defined by Riggenbach, and Andriopoulos, on TR of quasisyntomic commutative rings.
